# Club files organization report

## Scope and method

Organized copies of all 12 files from `input/` into topic folders under `output/`. The source files remain untouched. Similar or identical files were kept as separate copies; filenames and timestamps were not used to decide which proposal is final.

## Categories

- `communications/`: announcement text and poster text.
- `proposals/`: both proposal versions.
- `planning/`: meeting notes, next steps, rain plan, and draft budget.
- `supplies/`: both equipment files.
- `feedback/`: feedback questions.

## Verification performed
